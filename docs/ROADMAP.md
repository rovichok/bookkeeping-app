# Lentis Roadmap

## Overview

The Lentis roadmap outlines the planned evolution of the platform as it grows from a bookkeeping application into a broader business operations platform.

The roadmap is intended to communicate engineering direction rather than fixed delivery dates. Priorities may change as business needs evolve and new opportunities are identified.

The long-term objective is to build secure, maintainable software that simplifies business operations through automation, reporting, and workflow management.

---

# Guiding Principles

Future development will continue to emphasize:

- Solving real business problems
- Secure-by-default design
- Layered architecture
- Operational reliability
- Maintainable code
- Scalable system design
- Continuous improvement

Every major feature should support these principles.

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation ✅

Completed

Major accomplishments include:

- React frontend
- ASP.NET Core backend
- REST API architecture
- Entity Framework Core
- Production deployment
- JWT authentication
- HttpOnly cookies
- Cloudflare integration
- Security hardening
- Structured backend logging
- Engineering documentation

This phase established the technical foundation for future development.

---

# Phase 2 — Business Features

Planned enhancements include:

- Client Portal
- Expanded Lead Management
- User Profile Management
- Role-Based Authorization
- Administrative Dashboard Enhancements
- File Upload Support

These features strengthen the platform's core business capabilities.

---

# Phase 3 — Bookkeeping Automation

Future bookkeeping functionality includes:

- Cleanup Estimator
- Monthly bookkeeping workflows
- Client document management
- Financial reporting
- Task management
- Workflow automation

The objective is to reduce manual bookkeeping activities while improving operational efficiency.

---

# Phase 4 — Restaurant Operations

Restaurant-focused modules are planned to include:

- Vendor Management
- Invoice Processing
- OCR Document Capture
- Purchase Tracking
- Inventory Valuation
- Cost Analysis
- Operational Dashboards

These modules build upon real-world accounting and operational challenges observed in restaurant environments.

---

# Phase 5 — Integrations

Future integrations may include:

- QuickBooks Online
- Email services
- Calendar services
- Cloud storage providers
- Banking services
- Payment platforms

The goal is to reduce duplicate data entry by connecting existing business systems.

---

# Phase 6 — Analytics

Future analytical capabilities include:

- Business dashboards
- KPI reporting
- Financial trends
- Client performance
- Operational metrics
- Inventory analytics

Reporting should help business owners make informed operational decisions.

---

# Phase 7 — Artificial Intelligence

Long-term AI capabilities may include:

- Invoice classification
- OCR-assisted bookkeeping
- Automated transaction suggestions
- Workflow recommendations
- Financial insights
- Intelligent exception detection

AI should support human decision-making rather than replace professional judgment.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Lentis is intended to evolve into a modular business platform where individual components work together through a shared architecture.

Potential modules include:

- Client Management
- Vendor Management
- Bookkeeping
- Inventory
- Purchasing
- OCR Services
- Workflow Automation
- Analytics
- Reporting

Each module should remain independently maintainable while integrating seamlessly with the broader platform.

---

# Success Criteria

The platform will be considered successful if it:

- Solves meaningful business problems
- Reduces manual work
- Improves operational efficiency
- Maintains strong security
- Remains easy to maintain
- Continues to scale as new functionality is introduced

Technology choices should always support these objectives rather than become objectives themselves.

---

# Living Document

This roadmap is expected to evolve over time.

Completed items will move into project history, while new opportunities and priorities will be incorporated as the platform grows.

The roadmap should remain aligned with business needs, engineering principles, and lessons learned throughout the development of Lentis.

---

# Key Takeaways

- The roadmap focuses on long-term engineering direction rather than fixed dates.
- Future development is driven by real business problems.
- New features will build upon the existing architecture rather than replace it.
- Security, maintainability, and scalability remain guiding principles.
- Lentis is evolving into a modular business operations platform rather than a single-purpose application.
